App name
LAFORET SYLVIE FRANCOISE

Short description
Manage maintenance, bookings, tasks, and stock from one local operations board.

Full description
LAFORET SYLVIE FRANCOISE helps business teams organize daily facility work without accounts or cloud setup. Staff can track maintenance requests, assign task owners, schedule rooms, and monitor inventory levels from a focused Android workspace.

Records are handled on the device during the active app session, giving teams a practical operations board for the facility they manage. Dashboard summaries, status updates, detail views, and local settings keep the workflow clear during repeated daily use.
